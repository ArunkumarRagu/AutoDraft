  

\# Company Customer Support Knowledge Base

  

\## 1. Subscription Cancellation

  

Customers can cancel their premium subscription at any time.

  

To cancel a subscription:

  

1\. Log in to your account.

2\. Navigate to Settings.

3\. Select Subscription.

4\. Click Cancel Plan.

  

The subscription remains active until the end of the

current billing period.

  

\## 2. Refund Policy

  

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase.

  

Refund requests must be submitted through the

customer support portal.

  

Refunds are reviewed by the billing team.

Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

  

\## 3. Account Recovery

  

Customers who forget their password can recover

their account using the Forgot Password option.

  

A password reset link will be sent to their registered

email address.

  

If the customer does not receive the email, they should

check their spam folder.

  

\## 4. Payment Methods

  

We accept:

  

\- Credit cards

\- Debit cards

\- UPI

  

Customers can update their payment method from

Settings → Billing → Payment Methods.

  

\## 5. Technical Support

  

For technical issues, customers should first:

  

1\. Refresh the browser.

2\. Clear the browser cache.

3\. Try accessing the application using another browser.

  

If the issue continues, contact technical support

with the error message and a screenshot.
